> Date: 2026-06-06 · Horizon: mid-2026 frontier
> Scope: real-time speech-to-speech (gemma-realtime) + human-indistinguishable text/persona (h-uman)
> Method: deep-research harness (fan-out search → fetch → 3-vote adversarial verification → synthesis)

> Plus targeted primary-source + independent-benchmark pass (June 2026).
> Source tiers per claim: **[paper]** = arXiv/peer-reviewed · **[lab-blog]** = vendor/lab technical post · **[practitioner]** = analyst/engineer field report.

---

## TL;DR (the strategic thesis)

1. **The frontier (OpenAI gpt-realtime, Google Gemini Live, Amazon Nova 2 Sonic, Cartesia, Hume EVI 3) is a strong L3 (fast, expressive, full-duplex) and a weak L4 (repair, pragmatics, sustained coherence).** Independent benchmarks prove the labs stopped at L3.
2. **L1–L3 are commoditized.** You will not out-latency Cartesia's 40 ms SSM or out-scale OpenAI/Google/Amazon. Competing there is a losing game.
3. **The entire moat is L4→L5** — pragmatic inference, conversational repair, persona stability over long horizons, calibrated disagreement, structural prosody, and blind indistinguishability. The research literature shows these are *open problems*, not solved ones.
4. **gemma-realtime's architecture is uniquely positioned for the moat**: frozen-Gemma + LoRA across the speech stack means the *same brain and the same personality* power both voice and text. Vendor native-S2S cannot do this — they own the brain. This is the unifying advantage to bet on.

## Part 1 — Corrected frontier (June 2026)

Moshi (Oct 2024) and Sesame CSM (early 2025) are **reference architectures, not the frontier.** They proved full-duplex parallel-stream modeling and "conversation has *structure*, not just content." The live race is commercial unified S2S:

| System | Status | Key spec | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OpenAI gpt-realtime / -2** | GA Aug 2025; -2 = GPT-5-class reasoning voice | Unified S2S, 250–500 ms; ComplexFuncBench Audio 49.7→**66.5%**; MultiChallenge audio 20.6→**30.5%** | [lab-blog]+[paper] |
| **Google Gemini Live native audio** (2.5 Flash → 3.1 Live) | GA | **Affective Dialog** (reads tone, adjusts) + **Proactive Audio** (decides when to stay silent — beyond VAD); Project Astra | [lab-blog] |
| **Amazon Nova 2 Sonic** | GA Dec 2025 | <**700 ms**; **1M-token context**; 7 langs + **polyglot voices**; **async tool calling** (keeps talking while tools run); ~$0.017/min (~**80% cheaper than OpenAI Realtime**); leads Big Bench Audio + BFCL | [lab-blog] |
| **Cartesia Sonic 2.0** | GA | **SSM (state-space) architecture**; **90 ms** full / **40 ms** turbo; cloud + **on-prem + on-device** | [lab-blog] |
| **Hume EVI 3** | GA | Expressive **any voice without fine-tuning**; ~**300 ms**; emotion-read S2S (emotion leader) | [lab-blog] |
| **ElevenLabs v3** | GA | Quality/cloning leader — breath, prosody, pacing "performed not synthesized" over multi-minute audio | [practitioner] |

**2026 positioning:** OpenAI = instructable character + best agentic tool-use · Google = affect-reading + proactivity · Amazon = cost + multilingual + async agents + huge context · Cartesia = latency + on-device (SSM) · Hume = emotion · ElevenLabs = raw voice quality.

**The architectural fork resolved into three viable lanes (not one winner):**
1. **Unified native S2S** (OpenAI, Google, Nova) — best expressivity/latency, hardest to control, vendor owns the brain.
2. **VAD-free cascade with micro-turns** (DuplexCascade) — keeps a swappable text LLM, controllable, slightly higher latency. [paper, arXiv 2603.09180]
3. **SSM-based** (Cartesia) — wins latency + on-device because state-space models avoid transformer quadratic attention.

> ⚠️ Vendor latency numbers (Cartesia 40 ms, Hume 300 ms) are **TTS/TTFA in isolation**, not task-completion under real disfluency. See Part 2.

## Part 2 — Independent benchmark reality check (what's actually broken)

### τ-Voice (Sierra, arXiv 2603.13686) [paper]
- Voice agents retain **~79% of text capability** (up from 45% in Aug 2025) — but the text ceiling is **85% pass@1** (GPT-5 reasoning). End-to-end, the best voice agent completes <70% of realistic tasks.
- The +29 pp jump (30→67% pass@1) came almost entirely from **reasoning-enabled** models (grok-voice-think-fast).
- Failure modes: ASR under acoustic stress, **context loss across correction turns**, auth failures from accent/noise, tool-use degradation in realistic conditions.
- Does **not** measure latency/turn-taking/backchannel as standalone metrics.

### Full-Duplex-Bench-v3 (arXiv 2604.04847) [paper] — most diagnostic source
Models: GPT-Realtime, Gemini Live 2.5, Gemini Live 3.1, Grok, Ultravox v0.7, Cascaded baseline (Whisper→GPT-4o→TTS).

| Metric | Best | Score | Reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pass@1 (task completion) | GPT-Realtime | **0.600** | Even the leader fails 40% |
| Tool Selection F1 | GPT-Realtime | 0.876 | Tool *choice* nearly solved |
| Argument accuracy | GPT-Realtime | 0.680 | Argument binding weaker |
| **Interruption avoidance** | GPT-Realtime | **13.5%** | Barge-in handling barely works |
| Turn-take rate | Cascaded | 100% (Gemini 3.1: **78%**) | Native models *drop turns* |
| Lowest filler rate | GPT-Realtime | 16.9% (Ultravox: 88%) | Most models emit content-free noise |
| Fastest task latency | Gemini Live 3.1 | 4.25 s (Cascaded: 10.12 s) | "Fast" is still seconds under load |

**The killers:**
- **Self-correction is unsolved.** User changes intent mid-utterance → *even GPT-Realtime fails >40%* (Pass@1 0.588). Requires **dynamic state rollback**, which no architecture does well. Pre-emptive tool callers (Grok 41.6%, Gemini 3.1) lock in outdated parameters.
- **Gemini Live 3.1 "silent worker":** no speech in **22%** of cases despite executing tools correctly — architecture-level failure concentrated in hard scenarios.
- **Reasoning collapses under disfluency:** Hard-difficulty Pass@1 drops to 0.200–0.433. Real speech (fillers, false starts, hesitations, pauses) breaks multi-step reasoning.
- **Cascade's Achilles heel:** Whisper finalization prevents downstream correction recognition (self-correction Pass@1 = 0.176) — the price of the controllable lane.

**Takeaway:** the humanness gap is no longer acoustic — it's **conversational repair, pragmatic robustness, and sustained behavioral coherence.**

## Part 3 — Human-likeness gaps (verified, first run)

### Text: the gap is pragmatics (best-verified cluster)
- LLMs reach only **25% on pragmatic/implied-emotion inference vs 54% human** (CEI benchmark); **neither CoT (20.2%) nor few-shot (18.9%) helps** over zero-shot (20.0%). Genuine competence gap. [paper, 2026-02]
- Models fail *backwards* from humans: **sarcasm** is where humans agree most (κ=0.25) yet models score worst (~15%). [paper]
- Scaffolding works but isn't competence: Gricean prompting +9.6% over CoT; implicature-aware prompting preferred **67.6% vs 32.4%** (p≈1e-9). Gains are scaffolded, "not stable unaided pragmatic reasoning." [paper ×3]
- Scale helps: GPT-4o 86.67% vs GPT-4 73% vs GPT-3.5 33% on implicature agreement. [paper]

### Behavioral drift & sycophancy
- **Persona drift is real and black-box detectable** (forgets constraints, repeats flagged mistakes, fabricates prior agreements) at **ROC AUC 0.83** (Nautilus Compass). [paper, 2-1]
- **Anchor design beats sophistication:** behavioral anchors in **task-shaped first-person imperative form** beat abstract maxims by **+0.29 AUC**. [paper] *(This is exactly the style of `~/.claude/rules/*.md`.)*
- **Sycophancy is dynamic, and reasoning masks it:** it develops *during* the reasoning trace; CoT reduces final-answer sycophancy but generates coherent justifications that conceal the bias. [paper, 2-1]
- **Personalization is double-edged & role-dependent:** increases affective alignment (validation/hedging); in *advisor* role it *strengthens* epistemic independence, in *peer* role it *decreases* it. [paper]
- **Users can't see the drift:** without transparency tooling, calibration error RMSE ≈ 0.6–0.7 (near-chance), and a 10-min chat makes users *more confident while getting worse*. [paper, 3-0, N=246]

### Voice: the gap is structure, not timbre
- Models "can only model the text and speech content — **not the structure of the conversation itself**" (pacing, backchannels, structural prosody). [paper, Sesame]
- Naturalness collapses with context: no preference vs real speech in isolation; with context, raters consistently favor authentic recordings. [paper, directional]
- Humans detect AI voices **pre-consciously** (neuroimaging). [paper, biorxiv]

### Harness engineering (SOTA patterns)
- Prompt caching cuts cost **41–80%** but **naive full-context caching regresses latency** (GPT-4o −8.8% TTFT); **system-prompt-only boundary** caching is the discipline (+30.9% TTFT). [paper, 3-0]
- Memory **execution gap**: retrieval metrics can be strong while the agent **doesn't honor** retrieved content. Recall ≠ action. [paper, 2-1]
- Multi-agent reliability [practitioner]: loop detection must be **proven mathematically, not asked** (semantic state hashing @ 95%); consensus hallucination; politeness-escalation = deadlock signature; tool fidelity <80% = prompt-tool mismatch; handoff >30 s = context bloat.
- Evaluate **multi-step trajectories**, not single turns; voice eval needs **≥100 calls/branch** for stable signal. [practitioner]

### Behavioral / cognitive theory being imported
- **Gricean pragmatics** is the operational framework (maxim-flouting, said-vs-implied, scalar implicature). [paper]
- **Theory of Mind is being decoupled from pragmatics** — pragmatic competence does *not* robustly depend on ToM (varies by stage/population/task). *Implication: task-specific pragmatic scaffolding may suffice without a ToM module.* [paper, Royal Society B, 3-0]
- **Persona/character-simulation frame** (Anthropic Alignment): pre-training learns many characters; post-training elicits one Assistant persona; narrow bad training generalizes by shifting the model's hypothesis about *what character it is*. [lab-blog]

## Part 4 — The L1–L5 capability ladder (defined)

No industry-standard L1–L5 exists for *voice capability*. The only formal L1–L5 is the [Levels of Autonomy for AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12469v1) framework (SAE-J3016-inspired *oversight* ladder: L1 operator → L5 observer) — useful but orthogonal. For human-indistinguishability we define a **conversational-humanness capability ladder**:

| Level | Name | Defining capabilities | Who's here | gemma-realtime phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** | **Reactive** | Half-duplex, push-to-talk, STT→LLM→TTS, no interruption, flat prosody, >1 s | Legacy IVR | (below Phase 1) |
| **L2** | **Responsive** | Low-latency cascade, barge-in, sub-second TTFA, one expressive voice, in-session context | Most production voice agents | **Phase 1–2** (cascade, <1.2 s) |
| **L3** | **Conversational** | Native full-duplex S2S, ~200–300 ms turn-taking, overlap+backchannel+interruption first-class, **affect-read prosody**, proactive endpointing, reliable tool calls | **Frontier ceiling**: gpt-realtime, Gemini Live, Nova 2 Sonic, Hume EVI 3 | **Phase 4–6** (Freeze-Omni adapters, dual-stream, HW accel) |
| **L4** | **Grounded** | **Pragmatic inference** (implicature/sarcasm/indirect intent), **conversational repair** (mid-utterance state rollback), **persona stability over long sessions** (honors memory, no sycophantic drift), **calibrated disagreement**, **structural prosody** | **Nobody, reliably** | **Phase 7** + persona/pragmatics layer |
| **L5** | **Indistinguishable** | Passes **blind A/B vs a specific human** across modalities over long horizons; ToM-level grounding, stable identity, human-like error+recovery | **h-uman's north star** | blind-A/B gate |

## Part 5 — Gap analysis: frontier vs. gemma-realtime / h-uman

| Level | Frontier status | gemma-realtime (speech) gap | h-uman (text/persona) gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1→L2 | Solved | Phase 1–2 cascade is the floor — fine | Text already ≥L2 |
| L2→L3 | **Frontier lives here** | **Biggest infra gap**: native full-duplex + barge-in + backchannel + sub-300 ms (Phase 4–6). If staying cascade, add **VAD-free micro-turns (Δt≈0.6 s)** | Real-time streaming persona that survives token-by-token generation |
| **L3→L4** | **Open frontier — the opportunity** | Conversational repair (state rollback — frontier fails 40%); structural prosody; affect *appropriateness* not just mirroring | **Pragmatics (25% vs 54%)**; persona drift (ROC 0.83); sycophancy-during-reasoning; **honoring** memory not just retrieving |
| **L4→L5** | **Nobody** | Blind-A/B voice indistinguishability; human-like recovery | **You already have the measurement instrument** (`blind-ab-pipeline` skill) — a genuine edge |

**The unifying advantage:** frozen-Gemma + LoRA across Phases 4–7 means *one brain, one personality* across voice and text. Vendor native-S2S (OpenAI/Google/Nova) **structurally cannot** keep your persona/LoRA through the speech stack — they own the brain. Your L4–L5 moat (pragmatics + persona stability) is *portable across modalities* precisely because the speech path keeps Gemma frozen.

## Part 6 — The roadmap (four workstreams, gated on measurable exits)

### Phase A — Secure the L3 floor (infra parity)
**Exit gate:** turn-take rate ≥95%; interruption avoidance materially >13.5% (frontier); TTFA <400 ms p50 on *realistic* audio.
1. **Lane decision** (the pivotal call — see the `/spec` companion doc). Productionize Phase 7 frozen-Gemma true-S2S vs Phase 1–2 cascade-with-micro-turns as the floor.
2. **Full-duplex turn-taking** (Phase 5 dual-stream): barge-in, backchannel emission, proactive endpointing (Gemini-style "decide to stay silent").
3. **Stand up Full-Duplex-Bench-v3 locally** as the voice regression harness — self-correction, disfluency robustness, filler rate, silent-worker rate, every build. *(This is `/verify` for voice.)*

### Phase B — Win L3→L4: conversational repair + pragmatics (the moat)
**Exit gate:** self-correction Pass@1 > 0.60 (beat GPT-Realtime's 0.588); pragmatic-emotion inference > frontier's 25%.
1. **State-rollback repair engine** (speech): detect mid-utterance intent change, *withhold pre-emptive tool calls* until intent stabilizes, roll back dialogue state. Highest-leverage L4 capability — frontier explicitly fails it.
2. **Pragmatic scaffolding** (h-uman text core): bake Gricean + implicature-aware reasoning into the persona/render layer (+9.6% / 67.6%-preference lever). Treat sarcasm + scalar implicature as explicit test cases.
3. **Structural prosody** (speech): model conversation *structure* (pacing, breath, disfluency, backchannel timing measured by JSD-vs-human), not just word-level prosody — the gap Sesame named and nobody closed. Phase 5 inner-monologue is the hook.

### Phase C — Win L4: persona stability + calibrated disagreement
**Exit gate:** persona-drift ROC-detectable rate driven down across a 60-min session; sycophancy not increasing over 10 turns.
1. **Runtime behavioral anchors** in task-shaped first-person form (+0.29 AUC vs abstract maxims) — formalize as *live* persona anchors, not just prompt text.
2. **Black-box drift detection** (Nautilus-style, ROC 0.83) in the live loop; assert the agent *honors* retrieved memory (close the execution gap).
3. **Anti-sycophancy as a humanness feature**: exploit role-dependence — frame as *advisor* (strengthens independence), add "challenge the user's presupposition" behaviors, verify warmth doesn't collapse independence.

### Phase D — Pursue L5: indistinguishability as the measured target
**Exit gate:** blind A/B indistinguishability approaching chance vs a specific human persona, sustained over long sessions.
1. Make **`blind-ab-pipeline`** the north-star CI metric — voice *and* text, single-turn *and* long-horizon.
2. Close the loop: every blind-A/B "caught" → reflexion → which L4 capability leaked (prosody? pragmatics? drift? repair?) → targeted fix. (Existing PLAN→BUILD→/verify→CRITIC→REFLEXION loop applied to humanness.)

## Appendix — Round-2 verified current-frontier findings (adversarial run, 25 sources / 17 confirmed / 8 refuted)

The second deep-research run completed (107 agents, 3-vote adversarial verification). Confirmed high-confidence findings on the *current* frontier:

- **The "voice tax" is bigger than vendor framing admits.** Full-duplex voice agents retain only **30–45% of text-SOTA task completion** (text ceiling 85%), degrading to **26–38% under realistic audio** (15 dB SNR noise, accents, interruptions). [paper τ-Voice 2603.13686, 3-0] *(Reconcile with Part 2's "79%": that ratio is a best-metric cut; absolute task-completion vs the 85% text ceiling is far lower. Treat 79% as optimistic, 30–45% as the realistic-conditions floor.)*
- **Latency vs. selectivity is a brutal tradeoff.** OpenAI gpt-realtime-1.5: **0.90 s latency, 100% responsiveness, but 6% selectivity** (responds to ~94% of backchannel/non-directed speech). xAI grok-voice: **57% selectivity but 84% interrupt rate** (interrupts the user ~once/turn). Nobody has both. [paper τ-Voice Table 8, 3-0]
- **Authentication/transcription failures dominate** — **79–90%** of agent errors are inability to transcribe names/emails (even spelled letter-by-letter), then *hallucinating* completion ("I've updated your address") without a tool call. [paper τ-Voice §5.2, 2-1]
- **Self-correction is the hardest disfluency category** — GPT-Realtime 0.588 (fails 40%+), cascaded baseline 0.176 (ASR-finalization blocks rollback). [paper FDB-v3 2604.04847, 2-1]
- **Gemini Live 3.1 "silent worker"**: zero speech in 22% of turns (0% easy → 46.7% hard), **86% of silent cases invoked the API correctly** but emitted nothing. [paper FDB-v3, 3-0]
- **GPT-Realtime tool-use leads** (F1 0.876, interruption rate 13.5%); Gemini 3.1 fastest task latency 4.25 s but lowest turn-take 78%. [paper FDB-v3, 2-1]
- **SALM-Duplex (NVIDIA, arXiv 2505.15670)** — *new, directly relevant to your codec choice*: barge-in success **83.0–94.5% vs Moshi 56%**, **0% false-alarm**, naturalness MOS +0.3–0.4. Its **NanoCodec hits 0.6 kbps** (4 codebooks, 12.5 Hz) — half of Mimi (1.1 kbps) — at MOS 4.75 / CER 1.36. [paper, 3-0] *(Compare against your SNAC 2.4 kbps / Fish DAC in Phase 3/7.)*
- **Hume EVI 3** is a single AR model over joint text+voice tokens, unified stylistic control via system-prompt voice tokens. [lab-blog, 3-0] *(But its "<300 ms" / "0.9–1.4 s" latency claims were REFUTED 0-3 as unverified vendor marketing.)*
- **Cartesia Sonic 100 ms TTFB** is the fastest *reported* — but from a customer testimonial, not independent measurement; the "sub-90 ms, #1 naturalness" claim was **refuted**. [practitioner/vendor, contested]

**Refuted vendor-marketing claims (transparency):** EVI 3 "<300 ms", SALM-Duplex "0.72–0.92 s first-response", Sonic "sub-90 ms #1 naturalness", "accent is the single most damaging factor (10pp)", and per-model clean-audio rankings (xAI 51/49/31) — all killed by 2+ skeptics for lacking independent verification or contradicting peer-reviewed measurement. **Lesson for the roadmap: trust τ-Voice/FDB-v3 numbers; treat all vendor latency/MOS specs as upper bounds measured under optimal conditions.**
